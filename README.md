# Bajo Mundo AI Studio

A private, rights-aware AI video editing and original music creation workspace. Built for creators who own or have permission to edit their media.

The app connects to Runway Aleph 2.0 for video-to-video edits and Replicate/ElevenLabs Music for new soundtracks. It does not defeat copyright detection or transfer rights in third-party recordings.
